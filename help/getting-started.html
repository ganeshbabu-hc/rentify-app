Getting Started

Set Up Rentify the Right Way

Rentify is structured around a simple chain: Owner → Property → Rent Unit → Tenant → Billing. Once you understand that flow, the rest of the app becomes much easier to use.

Best first step: add one owner and one propertyThen add units and assign tenants

Intro Screens and First Launch

The first intro screen explains what the app does: property management, rent tracking, billing, reminders, reports, backups, and maintenance management.

You can set a name and title during onboarding. By default, the title starts as Property Manager. If you later sign in with Google, the app may use the Google account name for display where supported.

After intro, changing some profile identity fields may require Premium depending on the feature path.

Rentify encourages you to add your first property during onboarding. If you skip this step and still have zero properties later, the app can show a prompt on Home to help you get started.

Recommended sequence:

  1. Add an owner
  2. Add a property under that owner
  3. Add rent units inside the property
  4. Add a tenant
  5. Assign the tenant to a rent unit

How Navigation Works

The main bottom tabs give fast access to your core areas such as Home, Billing, Reports, and Profile-related flows depending on the current app structure.

Some tab transitions may show a loading animation for a smoother switch.

The drawer is the broader app menu. It groups related areas like Billing, Properties, Tenants, Owners, Rent Units, Maintenance, Reports, and Configure/Settings.

Use the drawer when you want structured navigation across management flows instead of quick dashboard-style access.

Many detail screens open as native stack screens. That means tapping a property, owner, tenant, rent unit, or bill can take you to a dedicated detail view with proper back navigation.

Profile Basics

The Profile page lets you manage your visible manager identity, display picture, and account-related actions. If you are using a local account, your custom name/title and local DP are used. If you are signed in through Google, Google account information may be used in some places.

Google sign-in is mainly useful for connected services such as Google Drive backup and purchase identity continuity. If you are not signed in with Google, local account behavior remains available.

Basic vs Premium

Premium can unlock items such as additional theme presets, more properties, and Google Drive backup/restore depending on the current app rules.

Rentify uses a Google Play Premium purchase flow. If you bought Premium earlier on the same Play account, use the restore action inside the app to re-check entitlement.
