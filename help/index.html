Welcome to Rentify

Detailed Help Center for Daily Property Management

Rentify helps you manage owners, properties, rent units, tenants, bills, reminders, recurring finance, reports, maintenance, backups, and premium features from one mobile app. This guide is written to be practical, detailed, and easy to use on mobile.

Rentify at a Glance

  • Owners can have bank details, UPI details, and PAN details.
  • Properties belong to owners and can contain multiple rent units.
  • Tenants are assigned to rent units with tenure and agreement dates.
  • Billing supports billable services, monthly setups, prorata logic, and partial payments.
  • Reports summarize collections, occupancy, and contribution patterns.
  • Backups support local files and Google Drive, with restore and scoped reset tools.
