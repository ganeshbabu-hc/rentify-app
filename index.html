Why property owners need Rentify

Rental management becomes messy when tenant records, rent bills, payment follow-ups, service charges, maintenance notes, and backups live in separate places. Rentify brings those daily jobs together so you can understand what is due, who paid, which unit needs attention, and what changed recently without rebuilding the same notes every month.

One connected record

Link tenants with rent units and properties, track deposits, lease dates, co-tenants, emergency contacts, and payment history from the same flow.

Billing without repeat work

Generate monthly bills, include rent, maintenance, utilities, additional services, partial payments, and invoice exports with less manual checking.

Clarity for decisions

Use reports, reminders, recent activity, and insights to spot pending payments, occupancy trends, regular payers, and units needing follow-up.

Everything a property portfolio needs

Rentify is designed for property owners who manage the work themselves: quick enough for daily updates, detailed enough for monthly billing, and safe enough to keep backups ready when phones change.

All-in-one overview

See properties, rent units, billing actions, and key stats from the home screen.

Smart monthly billing

Create bills, save drafts, track paid status, handle partial payments, and export invoices.

Connected portfolio

Keep tenants, rent units, owners, and properties linked so records stay consistent.

Reports and reminders

Review revenue trends, occupancy, top contributors, maintenance attention, and reminders.

Backup and restore

Use local and Google Drive backups, restore snapshots, and personalize app settings.

Built around the monthly rent cycle

Rentify helps you prepare bills with base rent, maintenance, billable services, utility charges, service requests, and drafts. When payments come in, you can record full or partial payments and keep tenant history updated for future reference.
